# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams evaluating Mahmoud Ghunaim for full-stack / front-end roles. They arrive from a job application, LinkedIn or a shared link, usually skimming on a laptop or phone between other candidates. Their job: decide within seconds whether he is credible (role, stack, real shipped work) and how to reach him.

Surfaces: the portfolio site (mahmoud-ghunaim.pages.dev) and the GitHub profile README (github.com/mahmoudGhunaim), which must read as one identity.

## Product Purpose

Turn a quick skim into an interview request. Success: a recruiter understands who he is (Full-Stack Developer — React, PHP, REST APIs, WordPress), sees proof (live projects, real experience at Softylus), and can contact him or download the CV in one click.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, modern. Speaks plainly and specifically; every element earns its place. Emotional goal: "this person ships clean work and knows what they're doing."

## Anti-references

- Widget soup: stacks of stats / trophy / streak cards that dominate the page and break when third-party services go down.
- Generic template look: the same capsule-render banner + typing-SVG layout thousands of GitHub profiles use.
- Emoji-bullet walls: every line prefixed with 🚀⚡🤝.
- Overclaiming: listing tools, levels or results that can't be backed up in an interview.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over adjectives** — show shipped projects and real roles instead of self-descriptions.
2. **Ten-second scan** — role, stack and contact are visible in the first screen.
3. **Every link works** — no dead links, broken images or placeholder URLs; prefer sources that don't fail.
4. **One identity** — the GitHub profile and the portfolio share name, title, colours and tone.
5. **Honest scope** — only claim skills evidenced by experience, projects or training.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: alt text on every image, meaningful link text, contrast that holds in both GitHub light and dark themes, respect reduced motion (no essential information inside animations).
